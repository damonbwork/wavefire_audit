Process / Control Narrative
Management follows ASC 606 for revenue recognition. All contracts are reviewed at inception by the Controller to determine performance obligations. Revenue is deferred until delivery milestones are met and confirmed by the operations team. Journal entries above $25,000 require dual approval from both the Controller and CFO. The Finance Director performs a monthly management review of all revenue accounts, comparing actuals to forecast and investigating variances greater than 5%.
